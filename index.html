FAQ

What is staking on Cardano?
Staking means delegating your ADA to a stake pool. The pool uses the combined stake to produce blocks on the Cardano blockchain, and every 5-day epoch the protocol automatically distributes rewards to the pool's delegators.
Are my ADA locked or at risk when I delegate?
No. Your ADA never leave your wallet: there is no lock-up period and you can send, spend or move them at any time. The pool operator never has access to your funds.
How do I delegate to SIGN?
Open any Cardano wallet that supports delegation (for example Daedalus, Lace or Eternl), search for the ticker SIGN in the staking or delegation tab and confirm the delegation. The steps above explain the whole process.
Is there a minimum amount or a cost to delegate?
There is no minimum amount. The first time you delegate, the wallet registers your stake key with a refundable deposit of 2 ADA, plus a small transaction fee.
When do I receive my rewards?
Your delegation becomes active at the start of the second epoch after you delegate, and the rewards for that epoch are paid at the end of the following one. In practice the first rewards arrive about 15–20 days after delegating, then automatically every 5 days.
What fees does SIGN charge?
SIGN currently has a 0% margin and a fixed cost of 170 ADA per epoch, the minimum allowed by the protocol. The fixed cost is shared by all delegators and is only deducted in epochs where the pool produces blocks.
Are rewards the same every epoch?
No. Rewards depend on how many blocks the pool produces, which is probabilistic: in some epochs a smaller pool may produce no blocks, in others more than expected, and over time the returns even out. You can estimate them with the official Cardano rewards calculator.
What are KES keys and why do you rotate them?
KES (Key Evolving Signature) keys are the hot keys the node uses to sign blocks. For security they expire after about 90 days (62 periods of 36 hours), so the operator regularly generates new KES keys and a new operational certificate. Every rotation is listed in the Updates section.
Can I change pool or stop delegating?
Yes, at any time and without penalties. A new delegation takes effect after two epochs. If you deregister your stake key, the 2 ADA deposit is returned to your wallet.
How can I check that SIGN is active?
Check the Updates section for the latest maintenance and KES rotations, and verify the pool's blocks, stake and delegators on Cexplorer or PoolTool.
